Fed Blackout Calendar

Windows in which Federal Reserve officials make no public remarks on monetary policy: from the second Saturday before each FOMC meeting through the Thursday after it. Scheduled economic data (CPI, NFP, PCE) still releases inside every window. Times are US Eastern.

Sources and updates

  • Federal Reserve, FOMC meeting calendars — dates verified 2026-09-16.
  • 2027 and 2028 meeting dates are tentative until confirmed at the meeting before.
  • When the Fed revises dates, this page and the subscribed calendar are rebuilt and re-pushed; both update themselves on the phone.
